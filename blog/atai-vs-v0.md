# Atai vs v0 (Vercel): UI generation vs building a business

People searching "Atai vs v0" or "v0 alternative" are often comparing two very different things without realising it. v0 is a generative UI tool; Atai is a business-building platform. This independent comparison (based on public positioning as of late 2026) explains when each makes sense.

## What v0 is good at

v0, by Vercel, generates user-interface components and pages from prompts, typically in React and Next.js. It's a favourite among developers who want production-quality UI scaffolding fast.

Where it shines:

- **Beautiful, on-brand UI** generated from a sentence or a screenshot.
- **Drop-in components** for teams already on React/Next.js.
- **Speed for front-end work** — great when the bottleneck is "build this screen."

If your need is "give me a polished component or page," v0 is hard to beat.

## What Atai is for

Atai takes a much wider view. Instead of generating a screen, it helps you turn a business idea into something you can run and grow:

- **Guided setup** of what the business sells and who it serves.
- **A built-in database** for the real data — customers, orders, content.
- **Growth tooling** — content, SEO, ads, referrals.
- **Human support** when you need a person, not just a model.

## Where Atai is the better fit

Consider Atai if you:

- Want a **complete business app**, not just a front end.
- Don't live in React and don't want to.
- Need **operations and growth** handled, not just design.
- Prefer **guidance** over a blank component canvas.

## Where v0 might be the better fit

v0 may suit you if you:

- Are a **developer** who needs UI fast and will wire everything else yourself.
- Already have a stack and just need **screens**.
- Want maximum control over the front-end code.

## How to decide

Ask: *do I need a screen, or do I need a business?*

- **Screen / component:** v0.
- **Business (build + run + grow):** Atai.
