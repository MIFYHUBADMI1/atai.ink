# Atai — Getting Started Guide

**A friendly, complete, step-by-step guide to starting, running, and growing your business on Atai.**

New to Atai? This guide walks you through everything in plain language — no coding, no jargon, no guesswork. You'll learn how to create your account, describe your business, build your app, publish it, operate it day to day, and grow it.

> **New here?** [Create your free account](/register) · Already have one? [Sign in](/login)

**What you'll need:** an email address (or a Google account) and a rough idea of the business you want to build. That's it.

**How long it takes:** about 2 minutes to sign up, 10–20 minutes to describe your business and review your first plan, and a few minutes to build. You can do it all in one sitting, or come back whenever you have time.

## 1. What Atai is

Atai is an **AI business-building platform**. You describe the business you want to run, and Atai helps you plan it, build the website or app it needs, host it, run it day to day, and grow it — all in one place, with AI teams doing the heavy lifting.

In one sentence: **you bring the idea and the decisions; Atai handles the building, hosting, and operations.**

Most tools stop at "here's a website." Atai is built around the whole job: planning the business, building the product, hosting it, serving customers, storing records, handling payments, and marketing what you sell.

### What you can do with Atai

- Describe your business idea in everyday language.
- Get a clear plan for who it's for, what you offer, and how it makes money — **before** anything is built.
- Create and host the website or app your business needs.
- Manage customers, records, and payments from one place.
- Use built-in tools to market your business and reach customers.
- Iterate on your product with plain-language instructions — no code.

### Who Atai is for

Atai is for people who have a clear idea but not a technical team:

- First-time founders and solo operators.
- Small business owners who want to take their business online.
- Freelancers and creators who want to sell a product or service.
- Anyone who can describe what they want but doesn't want to hire a developer.

### What Atai is not

Atai is **not** a get-rich-quick promise. It doesn't guarantee income, customers, or success. It's a set of powerful tools that makes building and running an online business much easier — but the decisions, the offer, and the effort are still yours. It also doesn't require you to know how to code.

### The journey at a glance

```
Imagine  →  Plan  →  Build  →  Launch  →  Operate  →  Grow
```

1. **Imagine** — decide what you want to build.
2. **Plan** — turn it into a clear, editable plan.
3. **Build** — turn the plan into a working app.
4. **Launch** — publish it so customers can use it.
5. **Operate** — run it: manage data, customers, and changes.
6. **Grow** — market it and reach more people.

Want the story behind it? Read [about Atai](/about) and the [founder's story](/founder).

## 3. Verify your email

Right after you sign up, Atai sends a verification email. This step is small but important.

1. Open the email and click the verification link — or go straight to the **[verification page](/verify-email)**.
2. If you don't see the email, click **Resend email**, wait a minute, and check your spam and promotions folders.

### Why verification matters

- **Your account unlocks after you verify.** Until then, you can look around but you can't build.
- **Your welcome credits are added on verification**, not at signup.
- It also protects your account and keeps your email accurate for support.

## 4. Your first project and the guided setup

After you verify, Atai runs a short, friendly setup conversation — usually under a minute. It's designed to shape your first plan, so your answers here matter.

### What it asks

- **What are you building?** One clear sentence is enough.
- **Who is it for?** Your audience — freelancers, small shops, students, and so on.
- **How do you want to start?** An idea, a website, or an existing codebase. (See [Section 5](#5-choose-how-you-want-to-start).)
- A few optional questions about your goals and experience.

You can **skip** optional questions. Your answers simply help Atai make a better first plan — nothing is locked in, and you can change everything later.

### What happens next

Atai creates your workspace and takes you to your first project, where you'll describe your business and review a plan. Nothing is built yet — and no build credits are spent until you approve the plan.

> **Tip:** If the setup ever fails, you can start again from the [start page](/start) — your input is usually preserved.

## 5. Choose how you want to start

You can start a new business at any time from your dashboard, or go straight to the **[start page](/start)**. There are three starting points, and each suits a different situation.

### An idea (recommended for beginners)

You have a business concept in plain words. This is the easiest place to begin, and it produces the most tailored plan. You'll answer a few focused questions about the problem you're solving, your audience, your solution, and how you plan to make money.

**Best for:** first-time founders, brand-new products, and anyone starting from scratch.

### A website

You want to use an existing site as a **reference** for the kind of business or experience you want — not to copy it. Atai reads the site, understands what it does, and builds a plan inspired by its structure and features.

**Best for:** modernizing an existing business, exploring a proven model, or reimagining a site you like.

> **Important:** Use a site as inspiration, not to copy someone else's work. Build something that's yours.

### An existing codebase (GitHub)

You already have a project on GitHub and want to continue, improve, or extend it. Atai reads the repository, understands the structure, and helps you build on it.

**Best for:** developers and technical users with existing code. If you're not technical, start with an idea instead.

## 6. Describe your business

The quality of your plan depends on the quality of your description. A specific sentence beats a vague one every time.

### The core questions

- **The problem you're solving** — the pain or gap your business addresses. This drives everything.
- **Your audience** — who you're helping (for example: freelancers, small shops, students).
- **Your solution** — how your product solves the problem, in plain language.
- **Your business model** — for example: subscription, one-time purchase, marketplace/commission, or free with paid upgrades.

### Vague vs. better

| Vague | Better |
|---|---|
| "A restaurant app" | "An app that helps small restaurants take orders without paying for an expensive POS system" |
| "Something for students" | "A study planner that turns a syllabus into a daily schedule students can check off" |
| "A shop" | "An online store for handmade leather goods aimed at commuters, with size guides and gift wrapping" |

### Tips for a strong description

- **Be specific about the customer.** "Small restaurants with fewer than 20 tables" beats "businesses."
- **Name the pain.** What goes wrong today without your product?
- **Keep it honest.** Describe what you can actually deliver, not a fantasy feature list.
- **Start small.** A focused product that does one thing well beats a sprawling one.

When you're ready, generate your plan. Atai takes you to a place where you can review and refine it before spending anything on a build.

## 7. Review and refine your plan

This is the most important step — and the good news is **nothing is built (and no build credits are spent) until you approve the plan.**

You'll see a split view: your **plan** on one side and an **AI chat** on the other.

### How to read the plan

- **What's being built** — the pages and features.
- **Who it's for** — the audience and their needs.
- **Key features** — what the app actually does.
- **The data it manages** — what information it stores (customers, orders, bookings, and so on).
- **How people sign in** — accounts and access.
- **How it makes money** — if you chose a paid model.

### How to ask for changes

You don't need technical language. Just say what you want, for example:

- "Remove the blog section — I only need a store and a contact page."
- "Add a page where customers can track their order."
- "Make the pricing page simpler, with just two plans."
- "Rename 'Bookings' to 'Appointments' everywhere."

The AI updates the plan and shows you the result. Keep going until it matches what you want.

> **Tip:** This is the cheapest place to make changes. A few minutes here can save a rebuild later, and rebuilds use more credits.

When you're happy, approve the plan to move on to building.

## 8. Build your app

Building turns your approved plan into a real, working application — pages, navigation, a database, user accounts, and everything else your business needs.

### What to expect

- Click **Start building** when you're ready.
- You'll see live progress as each stage completes.
- When it finishes, you'll have a working app you can preview.

### Good to know

- **Get the plan right first.** Small changes after a build may require rebuilding, which uses more credits.
- **If a build fails**, you can simply retry from your workspace. Your plan is kept, so you won't lose your work.
- **After the app exists**, make small changes with the **edit / instruction box** rather than rebuilding.

### While you wait

Builds can take a few minutes. Use the time to:

- Draft your first piece of content in the Content Studio.
- Think about who your first ten customers will be.
- Prepare any images or copy you want to add later.

## 9. Preview and test your app

Before you publish, take a few minutes to test your app like a customer would.

### A simple test checklist

- [ ] Open the home page — does it make sense at a glance?
- [ ] Click every menu item — do the pages load?
- [ ] Try the main action (buy, book, sign up, contact) — does it work?
- [ ] Sign up as a test user — does the flow make sense?
- [ ] Check it on your phone — is it readable and usable?
- [ ] Look for placeholder text or lorem ipsum that needs replacing.

### If something looks wrong

Describe the problem in plain language in the edit / instruction area — for example, "The 'Contact' button on the home page doesn't do anything," or "The checkout page is missing a total." Atai will make the change.

## 10. Publish and connect a domain

Building and publishing are two separate steps. Building creates the app; **publishing makes it live for customers.**

### Publish to a free Atai address

- Use the **Publish** button in your workspace.
- You'll get a **free web address** — no setup, no cost, and it works instantly.
- Your site is served securely over **HTTPS** automatically.

This is the fastest way to get live. You can connect your own domain later.

### Connect your own domain

A custom domain (like `yourbusiness.com`) looks more professional and builds trust.

1. Buy a domain from any domain provider if you don't already have one.
2. In your project, open the publishing or domain settings.
3. Copy the exact settings Atai shows you and add them at your domain provider.
4. Wait for the changes to take effect — this can take a few minutes to a few hours.
5. Come back and confirm.

> **Tip:** If your domain doesn't connect right away, it's usually just a waiting game. Double-check you copied the settings exactly, then try again later.

### Re-publishing

When you make changes and want them live, publish again from your workspace. Always make sure the latest build is the one that's live.

## 11. Operate and manage your business

Once your app is live, day-to-day work happens in a few simple areas.

### Your dashboard

Your dashboard is your command center: it shows your businesses, recent activity, and shortcuts to create something new. Think of it as your home base.

### The workspace

Each business has a workspace — the home page for that project. It shows the current state, build controls, a preview, and your publishing history. When you're not sure where to go, start here.

### The Database Manager

Every app comes with a built-in database you can view and edit yourself. Think of it as spreadsheets behind the scenes: each **table** holds one kind of thing (like Customers or Orders), and each **record** is one row.

- **View** what your app is storing.
- **Add, edit, or delete** records.
- **Link** related records (for example, an order to the customer who placed it).

You don't need to write code or queries. → [Read the Database Manager guide](/docs/database-manager)

### The AI co-founder

You can ask the AI co-founder questions at any time — "How do I grow this week?", "What does this page do?", "Help me improve my plan." It can explain things and point you to the right place. Think of it as a knowledgeable teammate who's always available.

### Making changes

To change your app after it's built, describe the change in plain language in the edit / instruction area. For bigger changes, you may need to update the plan and rebuild.

### Keeping track of versions

Every build and publish is a version. If something goes wrong, you can usually go back to an earlier working version. Don't be afraid to experiment — you can always roll back.

### Growth overview

Your starting point. It brings together market research, competitors, launch status, and a short list of recommended next steps. If you're not sure what to do this week, start here.

- **Brand** — set a consistent voice and look.
- **Calendar** — plan and schedule posts.

A simple routine: draft in Studio, then promote your best pieces.

Checks your published site for search-engine readiness and gives you a prioritized list of fixes (titles, descriptions, structure, speed). Run it after you publish, and again after big changes.

Before spending on ads, answer: who is this for, what do competitors offer, and what's my angle? This helps you avoid wasting money on the wrong message.

## 13. Credits and billing

Atai uses **credits** for actions like analyzing a website, generating a plan, building, and publishing. A subscription gives you a monthly credit allowance and unlocks the capabilities of your plan; you can also buy permanent credits that never expire. It keeps things simple and predictable.

### The five plans

Atai has five plans. You can start free and upgrade when you're ready to launch. **The [Pricing page](/pricing) always shows the current prices and credit allowances** — the numbers below are a guide, and monthly credit allowances are provisional and may be adjusted.

| Plan | Price | Best for | What you get |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Explorer** | Free | Mapping out your idea | Plan with the AI co-founder, research markets and competitors, **500 planning credits** on email verification, community support. **Planning only** — you can't launch or host a production business on Explorer. |
| **Starter** | $89.99/mo · $989.89/yr | Launching your first real business | **1 business** (1 production app on your Atai subdomain), ~150,000 credits/mo, AI co-founder, specialist teams, SEO & content marketing, business-intelligence basics, standard support. |
| **Growth** ⭐ | $199.99/mo · $2,199.89/yr | Running and growing several businesses | **Up to 4 businesses**, **1 custom domain**, ~400,000 credits/mo, advanced AI co-founder, expanded teams, ads & competition monitoring, full learning paths, direct support. **Most popular.** |
| **Scale** | $499.99/mo · $5,499.89/yr | Operating at scale | **Unlimited businesses**, **up to 4 custom domains**, ~1,200,000 credits/mo, premium AI co-founder, premium specialist teams, the full business-intelligence suite, priority compute, premium support. |
| **Enterprise** | Custom | Large or regulated operations | Everything in Scale, tailored: custom credit volume, dedicated infrastructure, SLA-backed uptime, white-glove onboarding, a dedicated account manager. Contact sales. |

> **Custom domains:** a custom domain (like `yourbusiness.com`) is included with Growth (1) and Scale (up to 4). Additional domains can be purchased separately. Every paid plan also includes a free Atai subdomain.

### Monthly or annual billing

Paid plans can be billed **monthly** or **annually**. Annual billing is **12 months for the price of 11** — about **8% saved** versus paying monthly. You're charged once for the year, and your **credits are still issued every month**: your monthly allowance refreshes each month, just like a monthly plan. Choose the cycle on the [Pricing page](/pricing) or in **Settings → Billing**.

### How credits work

- **Two kinds of credits.** *Subscription credits* come with your plan each month and expire at the end of that billing month. *Permanent credits* are bought as one-time packs and **never expire**.
- **Subscription credits are spent first**, then permanent credits — so your monthly allowance is used before your purchased stash.
- **Welcome credits:** new accounts receive **500 credits after verifying email** — enough to explore and plan, not necessarily to launch a full business.
- **Every action is reserve → execute → charge or refund.** Atai reserves the cost before it starts, then charges the real amount and refunds any difference. A failed action refunds automatically, and you can never be double-charged for one operation.
- **Earn free credits:** invite friends through **[Referrals](/referrals)** — you earn credits when they verify their email, and a bonus when they reach a usage milestone.

### Permanent credit packs (never expire)

Buy these any time in **Settings → Billing**, on top of any plan:

| 500,000 *(popular)* | $119 |

### Managing your billing

Everything lives in one place: **[Settings → Billing](/settings/billing)**.

- **Check your balance and history:** the Credit Balance card and Credit History table show every transaction.
- **Buy more or upgrade:** buy permanent credits or change your plan there. Compare options on the **[Pricing page](/pricing)**.
- **Change or cancel a plan:** open your subscription in Settings → Billing and change or cancel it. Cancelling never deletes your data or your businesses.
- **Payment problems:** if you paid but don't see credits, refresh, check your credit history, then email **support@atai.ink** with your receipt.

> **Tip for your first project:** start with the standard build option and refine your plan carefully before building. This keeps costs down while you learn how everything works. See [Appendix A](#appendix-a-billing-and-credits-reference) for the full action-by-action cost table.

## 14. Account and security

Your account settings live in one place, and a few simple habits keep you safe.

### Profile

Update your name, photo, and email in your profile settings. If you change your email, you'll need to confirm it.

### Password and sessions

- Change your password any time in security settings.
- See where you're signed in, and sign out of devices you don't recognize.
- Sessions expire after a while for your protection — just sign in again.

### Connected accounts

You can connect services like Google or GitHub. If you ever want to disconnect, you can do that from the same place — disconnecting stops future use but doesn't delete anything you've already created.

### Good habits

- Use a strong, unique password (or sign in with Google).
- Don't share your password with anyone.
- Sign out on shared devices.

You're never on your own. If you get stuck:

1. **What you were trying to do.**
2. **What happened instead** (paste the exact error text if you can).
3. **When it happened** (roughly).
4. **Your project name** (not your password — never send passwords).

That's it — with those four things, support can usually help quickly.

## 16. Common problems and how to solve them

Most issues are quick to fix. Find your problem below.

### I can't log in
- *Cause:* wrong password, unverified email, or a closed account.
- *Fix:* use [Forgot password](/forgot-password) to reset, or [sign in](/login) with Google. If you never verified your email, do that first at the [verification page](/verify-email).

### My verification email never arrives
- *Fix:* on the [verification page](/verify-email), click **Resend email**, wait a minute, and check spam and promotions. Still nothing? Contact support.

### It says "verify your email" and I feel locked out
- *Cause:* your account unlocks after you verify your email, and welcome credits are added then.
- *Fix:* open the verification link in your welcome email, or use [Resend email](/verify-email).

### I keep getting logged out
- *Cause:* sessions expire after a while for security.
- *Fix:* sign in again. You can review active sessions in your security settings.

### "Not enough credits"
- *Cause:* your balance is lower than what the action needs.
- *Fix:* check **Settings → Billing** to see your balance and options, or earn free credits through referrals. See [pricing](/pricing). Use the standard build option for your first project, and refine your plan before building.

### Credits were taken but the action failed
- *Fix:* check your credit history in **Settings → Billing**. If a charge doesn't have a matching refund, contact support with your project name and the time it happened.

### I paid but don't see my credits
- *Fix:* refresh the page. If you returned from checkout and still don't see them, check your credit history, then contact support with your receipt.

### My project is stuck on "Analyzing"
- *Cause:* analyzing a website can take several minutes.
- *Fix:* wait a little, then refresh. If it's been very long, use the retry option in your workspace.

### My plan won't generate
- *Cause:* the website blocked access, or the description was too vague.
- *Fix:* try a clearer description or a different website, then retry.

### My build failed
- *Cause:* a temporary error, or the plan was very large.
- *Fix:* use **Retry** in the workspace. If it keeps failing, simplify the plan or try the more powerful build option.

### Small changes after building cost a lot
- *Cause:* changing the plan after building re-runs the build.
- *Fix:* get the plan right before building. After building, make small changes with the edit / instruction box instead of rebuilding.

### My published site shows an old or broken version
- *Fix:* refresh the page and re-publish from your project. Make sure the latest build is the one that's live.

### My custom domain won't connect
- *Cause:* the domain settings haven't finished updating, or the records are wrong.
- *Fix:* copy the exact settings Atai shows, wait for them to take effect, then try again. Free Atai addresses need no setup.

### A table says "not found"
- *Fix:* open **Database** in your project and choose the table from the list. See the [Database Manager guide](/docs/database-manager).

### I can't edit the ID or timestamps
- *Fix:* those are managed by Atai — this is expected. Add your own text field if you need a reference number.

### "Failed to save" a record
- *Fix:* check each field matches its type (for example, numbers where numbers belong) and fill in required fields, then save again.

### A growth tool is greyed out or says "Coming soon"
- *Fix:* check Settings for a toggle. If you can't enable it, it isn't available for your account yet — check back later or contact support.

### The SEO audit won't run
- *Cause:* your project isn't published yet, so there's no live site to check.
- *Fix:* publish your project first, then run the audit.

### I'm being "rate limited"
- *Cause:* too many requests in a short time.
- *Fix:* slow down and retry after a moment. Check your usage in your project settings if it keeps happening.

## Frequently asked questions

### Can I change my mind after building?
Yes. You can refine your plan, make small changes with the instruction box, or rebuild from an updated plan. Nothing is permanent.

### Can I use my own domain?
Yes. You can publish to a free Atai address or connect your own custom domain. See [Publish and connect a domain](#10-publish-and-connect-a-domain).

### Is my data mine?
Yes. Your data lives in your project's database, which you can view, edit, and export. See the [Database Manager guide](/docs/database-manager).

### Do I need to know how to code?
No. You describe what you want in plain language and Atai builds it. If you want to go deeper, the [Runtime & SDK guide](/docs/runtime-api) is there for developers.

### How much does it cost?
Credits power actions like building and publishing. See [pricing](/pricing) for current plans and options.

### Can I start more than one business?
Yes. Your dashboard holds all your businesses, and you can start a new one any time from the [start page](/start).

### Does Atai guarantee customers or income?
No. Atai gives you powerful tools, but success depends on your offer, your market, and your effort. We're honest about that.

### How do I cancel?
You can change or cancel your plan in Settings → Billing. See the [refund policy](/refund-policy) for details.

### What if I get stuck?
Email support@atai.ink or read the [troubleshooting guide](/docs/troubleshooting). Include your project name and the exact message you saw.

### Can I invite other people to help?
Yes — look for team or collaboration options in your project settings. Invite people you trust, and be careful about who can make changes.

### Will my app work on phones?
Yes. Apps built by Atai are designed to work on phones, tablets, and desktops. Always test on your own phone before you publish.

### How long does a build take?
Usually a few minutes. More complex apps can take longer. You can keep working while it builds.

## Tips and common mistakes

### Common mistakes to avoid

- **Skipping email verification.** It unlocks your account and your welcome credits.
- **Rushing the plan.** The plan is the cheapest place to make changes.
- **Building before the plan is right.** Rebuilds cost more than plan edits.
- **Being vague in your description.** Specific beats general, every time.
- **Ignoring the SEO audit.** A little tuning helps people find you.
- **Sharing your password or keys.** Keep them private.
- **Deleting data without a backup.** There's no undo for a deleted table.
- **Publishing without testing.** Click everything like a customer first.

### Getting the most from your first build

1. **Start with one clear product.** A focused app beats a sprawling one.
2. **Test like a customer.** Click everything before you publish.
3. **Publish early.** A live site you can improve beats a perfect one that never ships.
4. **Ask for feedback.** Invite a friend to try it and tell you what confused them.
5. **Improve weekly.** Small, consistent changes compound.
6. **Use the growth overview.** It tells you what to do next.

### A.4 How credits actually work

- Two types: **subscription** (monthly, expire at period end) and **permanent** (never expire).
- Consumption order: **subscription credits first, then permanent** — your monthly allowance is used before your purchased stash.
- Every operation uses **reserve → execute → charge/refund** with atomic reservation and idempotency keys. No race conditions, no double charges, automatic refund on failure.
- Full audit trail in the credit ledger; entries carry types such as `signup_bonus`, `subscription_grant`, `build_reservation`, `build_refund`, `credit_purchase`, `fork_purchase`, `ai_collaboration`, and `runtime_usage`.
- Free credits: **500 welcome** (on email verification), **500 referral verification reward**, **1,500 referral milestone reward**.
- If your balance is too low, a **Credit Gate dialog** appears with tailored upgrade options instead of a dead end.

## Appendix B: Project lifecycle statuses

Every business has a state, persisted server-side. You'll see these as plain-language labels in the dashboard and sidebar.

| State | Label you see | What it means / what to do |
|---|---|---|
| `created`, `pending_plan` | Getting started | Open the project or ask Atai |
| `analyzing` | Understanding | Atai is reading your website/idea. Deep crawls can take up to 15 minutes. Watch [progress](https://atai.ink/project/PROJECT_ID/progress) |
| `analysis_complete` | Ready to plan | Continue to the workspace |
| `analysis_failed` | **Understand needs attention** | Retry the Understand step from the workspace |
| `specification_ready`, `plan_ready` | **Plan ready — review before building** | Go to Collaborate, review & refine |
| `plan_failed` | **Plan needs attention** | Retry the Plan step from the workspace |
| `awaiting_build_confirmation` | Awaiting build confirmation | Click **Start building** on Collaborate |
| `building` | Building application | The build is running; a stage overlay shows progress |
| `build_complete`, `ready` | Ready / Build complete | App exists; publish or iterate |
| `build_failed` | Build failed | Retry build from the workspace — the plan stays editable |
| `deploying` | Deploying | Publishing to production |
| `deployed` | Deployed | Live |
| `deployment_failed` | Deployment failed | Republish from Hosting |

> **Important:** once the state is `building`, `deploying`, `ready`, `build_complete` or `deployment_failed`, **the plan becomes a read-only archive**. Editing it there no longer reaches the built app. All later changes go through the follow-up edit flow ([/edit](https://atai.ink/project/PROJECT_ID/edit)) or the instruction bar. `build_failed` deliberately keeps the plan editable because a retry re-sends the whole plan.
