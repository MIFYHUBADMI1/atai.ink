# Atai — Product Claims vs Implementation (Pre-MVP Audit, Part 6)

## Scope & method

Read-only review of the marketing surface (`app/page.tsx` → `components/landing/*`, `app/pricing/page.tsx`, `app/about`, `app/founder`, `app/start`, `app/new`, `app/docs` + `components/docs-content.tsx`, `app/sdk`, `app/developers`, `app/modes-comparison`, legal pages) against the implementation surface (`lib/billing/*`, `lib/analysis/*`, `lib/credits/*`, `lib/planning/*`, `lib/integrations/totalum/*`, `lib/runtime/*`, `app/api/*`, `components/workspace/*`). Every concrete promise was traced to code that delivers it or declared unbacked. Billing arithmetic was checked against `lib/billing/config.ts` and `lib/billing/runtime-config.ts`. No state-mutating actions were taken; no build or test suite was run. Line references are from the working tree at audit time.

## Dead ends and broken affordances

- **Coming-soon pages wired into live navigation.** 13 page files render `ComingSoonPage`/`ProjectComingSoon` (`app/ads`, `app/market`, `app/lessons`, `app/hosting`, `app/competition`, `app/project/[id]/{ads,tasks,studio,market,market/seo,lessons,campaigns,competition}`). The nav marks five entries `kind:"soon"` (`components/workspace/atai-nav.ts:75,77,79,101-110,116`), and the sidebar renders a "Soon" badge (`components/workspace/atai-sidebar.tsx:60-62`) — honest — but the landing/about pages advertise the underlying capabilities (marketing, growth, competition) as present.
- **Hosting is inconsistent with itself.** Global `/hosting` is ComingSoon (`atai-nav.ts:116`), yet the per-project `/project/[id]/hosting` page is fully real (`app/project/[projectId]/hosting/page.tsx` uses `PublishMenu` + `DeploymentHistory`). A user who clicks "Hosting" from the global nav gets "soon"; from a project they get the feature.
- **Google "Coming soon" on the profile page while Google OAuth is live.** `app/settings/profile/page.tsx:191-213` shows a greyed "Coming soon / Soon" Google box; meanwhile `/api/auth/google/*` exists, the signup panel renders a working "Continue with Google" (`components/auth/auth-panel.tsx:186-191`), and account-linking for password users is implemented (`lib/auth/users.ts:162-200`). An existing password user is told a live capability is unavailable.
- **"Archived" tab can never populate.** `app/projects/workspace.tsx:114-118` — `matches: () => false`, comment: "placeholder for future". Users will click it, see an empty state, and conclude their data vanished.
- **Nav item pointing at a raw API route.** "Export snapshot" is a nav entry to `/api/projects/${projectId}/export` (`atai-nav.ts:55`) — an internal endpoint rendered as primary navigation (harmless-looking but not a page).
- **Plan buttons that do nothing for some states.** `components/billing/plan-card.tsx:183-188`: when logged in on Free, the Free card renders either "Current Plan" disabled or `null` — fine; but `PlanCard` click-to-select (`:102-111`) toggles a "Selected" ring on the whole card, a state that only changes the subscribe label and has no other effect — an affordance that looks like choice but does nothing except restyle.
- **Terminology drift on the same nouns.** Nav "All projects" (`atai-nav.ts:100`) vs delete toast "Business deleted" (`app/projects/workspace.tsx:145`) vs CTA "Open my businesses" (`components/landing/landing-page.tsx:24`) vs "Creating your Atai workspace" (`app/start/page.tsx:21`) vs routes `/project/*`. Three adjacent nav entries "Collaborate", "Plan", "Edit plan" (`atai-nav.ts:47-49`) plus "Workspace" and "Team progress" describe one pipeline to a user who cannot tell which is which.

## Trust gaps

- **No receipt or invoice surface.** The only transactional emails are verification, password reset, and email change (`lib/email/templates.ts:283-343`; importers: `lib/auth/verification.ts`, `app/api/auth/forgot-password`, `app/api/auth/change-email`). `lib/billing/dodo-webhook.ts` sends nothing on `succeeded`/`refunded`. If Dodo emails receipts on its own, that's outside this repo's control — unverified.
- **No post-payment confirmation.** Checkout returns to `/settings/billing` or the origin page (`lib/billing/checkout-return.ts:3-11`); no page consumes a payment-status query param and `app/settings/billing/page.tsx` renders no payment/invoice history (grep: no "payment|invoice|receipt" rows). A card gets charged and the user lands on a settings page with a plan label as the only evidence.
- **"Support through the platform" doesn't exist.** `app/refund-policy/page.tsx:363` says "Reach Atai support through the platform or call +256 761 819 885". The platform's "Help & support" nav entry goes to `/docs` (`atai-nav.ts:122`) — static docs, no contact form or ticket. The real channels are one shared email and one personal WhatsApp number (terms `app/terms/page.tsx:720-728`), also printed response targets ("1–2 business days", `:731") nobody in code can meet.
- **Legal pages vs behavior.** Privacy discloses providers honestly (Totalum, Firecrawl, Dodo; `app/privacy/page.tsx:554-557`) — good — but lists "Resend" while the mailer is generic SMTP (`lib/env.ts:57-76`, minor). Terms is materially false on plans/pricing (see table #22) while simultaneously containing the honest no-guarantee language (`app/terms/page.tsx:648-649`) — the disclaimers and the product table contradict each other.
- **Refunds are manual promises.** `refund_reversal`/`admin_adjustment` ledger types exist (`lib/billing/config.ts:361-363`) and admin tooling supports it, but there is no customer-facing refund request path; the policy page promises processing "within 48 hours of approval" with no intake mechanism besides the shared email.
- **"About six simple launches' worth of work"** and "one simple launch a month" papers over the real economics: a $12 Explorer month buys ~1 build, publish costs extra, and chat costs 2 credits/message (`lib/billing/runtime-config.ts:29-33`) while a build costs 50,000 — a 25,000x spread a first-timer cannot reconcile with "500 free credits".

## Confirmed findings

Refs PROD-01…; severity P0 (launch blocker) → P3 (cosmetic/risk).

- **PROD-01 · P0 · launch blocker — Terms of Service invents a pricing table that doesn't exist.** `app/terms/page.tsx:460-468` sells Free/Explorer/Launch/Growth/Scale/Enterprise at $20/$99/$399/$599 with project caps, build caps, 30-day trial and team seats, contradicting `lib/billing/config.ts:98-253` and `/pricing`. Fix: **replace the table with a pointer to `/pricing` and remove the fabricated plan rows** (do not build the described limits).
- **PROD-02 · P1 — Three contradictory hosting-payment claims, none implemented.** `app/pricing/page.tsx:122,604` ("no hosting bill, free forever") vs `:647` ("first six months included, then a line in your plan") vs `components/publish-menu.tsx:471` ("Hosting free for 6 months, then paid"). No billing path charges for hosting after any period. Fix: pick the truth (free hosting for deployed apps is what the code does), **delete the six-month line from pricing copy and publish-menu**.
- **PROD-03 · P1 — Plan feature matrix is decorative.** Free/Explorer show X'd-out features ("Use your own domain", "Download source code", "Built-in code editor") that are technically available to every user because no route checks subscription state (verified across `app/api/projects/[id]/**`, `components/publish-menu.tsx`). Either gating is a promise you don't keep commercially (users get paid-tier capability free) or the copy lies about restrictions. Fix: state plainly that plans differ by **credits included**, remove fake not-Included X lists, or implement gating (not recommended pre-MVP).
- **PROD-04 · P1 — "Priority builds" and "Up to 30 parallel AI agents" have zero implementation.** `lib/billing/config.ts:146,159,185,204`; no queue-priority or concurrency feature exists. Fix: **remove both lines from the config and the pricing translations** (`app/pricing/page.tsx:180,196-197`).
- **PROD-05 · P2 — Support tiers promise teams that don't exist.** "A support team behind your builds, not just a form" (`app/pricing/page.tsx:60`), "Talk to a person directly" (`:67`). Actual support: `support@atai.ink` + personal WhatsApp (`app/terms/page.tsx:720-728`). Fix: reword to "direct email/WhatsApp support" or build a ticket intake; until then Business/Professional differentiators are hollow.
- **PROD-06 · P2 — `/modes-comparison` publishes stale costs and a fabricated quality statistic.** `app/modes-comparison/page.tsx:53-60,112-119,148`: "~10 credits", "50-100 credits", "30-60 seconds", "Up to 200% better quality" — real defaults are 5/100 (`lib/billing/runtime-config.ts:67-74`); timings/quality are unmeasured; mode names don't match code ("legacy"/"heavy") nor the pricing page's 50,000-credit build framing. Fix: delete the page or rewrite numbers from runtime-config and drop the 200% claim.
- **PROD-07 · P2 — Billing values hardcoded across UI and grant paths despite an admin-config system.** `WELCOME_BONUS_CREDITS`/500 literals at `components/billing/plan-card.tsx:164`, `app/pricing/page.tsx:171`, `components/landing/sections.tsx:427`, `components/docs-content.tsx:189`, `app/terms/page.tsx:446`; duplicates at `lib/auth/users.ts:7` and `lib/store/mongo-store.ts:19` while `grantWelcomeBonus` (`lib/billing/credit-service.ts:1031-1046`) reads admin runtime config — two paths can even grant **different amounts** to different users. Deploy cost 500 is likewise hardcoded in pricing copy (`app/pricing/page.tsx:486,616,657`). Fix: single source (`lib/billing/config.ts` + runtime-config read server-side); this violates the repo's own rule at `config.ts:4`.
- **PROD-08 · P2 — Profile page marks Google "Coming soon" while Google sign-in/linking is live.** `app/settings/profile/page.tsx:191-213` vs `app/api/auth/google/*` + `lib/auth/users.ts:101-200`. Fix: replace the disabled box with a real "Connect Google" action (backend already exists) or hide the row entirely.
- **PROD-09 · P3 — Dead "Archived" tab** in `app/projects/workspace.tsx:114-118` (`matches: () => false`). Fix: hide until an archived state exists.
- **PROD-10 · P3 — Capability advertised while flag-off.** Landing/about describe growth/marketing teams; Marketing Studio is allowlist-gated (`app/project/[projectId]/studio/page.tsx:53-60`) and campaigns never spend (`route.ts:20-28`). Fix: gate the marketing sections of `sections.tsx`/`about-content.tsx` behind the same flag, or soften to "coming".
- **PROD-11 · P2 — No payment confirmation artifacts.** No receipt email, no payment history UI, no success screen (see Trust gaps). Fix (minimal): a "Payment received — check your plan" state on checkout return plus a payments list from the existing webhook-stored records; confirm Dodo's own receipt emails before shipping copy that implies them.
- **PROD-12 · P3 — "Funding Support AI team" (about page) has no backing at all.** `app/about/about-content.tsx:16`. Fix: remove the card.
- **PROD-13 · P3 — Credit scale is not explainable in one sitting.** Chat 2, plan 5, deep crawl 500, deploy 500, build 50,000, welcome 500 (`runtime-config.ts:29-74`, `config.ts:55-74,287`); pricing calls 500 credits "enough to try the work" without ever saying it is 1% of a launch. Terminology rotates project/business/workspace/app across `atai-nav.ts`, `workspace.tsx:145`, `app/start/page.tsx:21`. Fix: one glossary moment in onboarding ("your 500 credits buy X minutes of chatting and planning; a launch costs 100× that — here's why") and pick one noun.
- **PROD-14 · P3 — "One offer, online this week"** (`app/pricing/page.tsx:86`) has no supporting code or SLA. Fix: reword to "in days, not months, once credits are available" or attach an honest status-page metric.

## What a first-time founder experiences today

They land on atai.ink: honest, non-hyperbolic copy ("not to promise income", `components/landing/sections.tsx:369`) with 500 free credits and a working composer. They register, verify email (credits granted via `lib/auth/users.ts:205`), and are walked through `/start` into `/project/[id]/collaborate` — a genuinely nice pipeline. They chat (2 credits/message), maybe deep-crawl a reference site (500 — a **full** welcome bonus in one click, with no pricing warning in the crawl dialog beyond a small "500 credits" pill, `components/crawl-mode-dialog.tsx:101`), and get a plan (5 credits). Then the plan page quotes the build at **50,000 credits** — 100× everything they've been told free credits buy. `/pricing` explains plans as "about one simple launch a month" for $12, which is technically honest arithmetic but collides with: X'd features that aren't actually locked, a "6 months then paid" hosting line contradicting "free forever" two sections above, and a separate `/modes-comparison` and terms table describing another product's numbers. If they pay, Dodo checkout works, they land back at `/settings/billing` with **no confirmation, no receipt, no email**, and a ledger only they can see. They look for support: "Business" plan promised "a support team, not just a form"; they find `/docs` and a shared email with a personal Ugandan WhatsApp number in the terms. They click Competition, Ads, Lessons, Market, Hosting (global), or Archived: "Soon." They click Google connect on their profile: "Coming soon" — while their friend signed up with Google an hour ago. The core build→deploy→host loop they were sold is real; almost every *tier, restriction, and support* promise wrapped around it is not.
